# Questions

## Basic

* Retrieve the total number of orders in the table.
* Display the first 10 rows of the table.
* Describe the table structure (columns and data types).
* Count the unique Order IDs.
* Check for missing values in Order ID, Order Time, Delivery Time, Pizza Type and Location.
* Identify the months that bring the most orders, ranked from high to low.
* List the monthly orders in chronological order, from January to December.
* List the top 5 most ordered pizza types.
* Count the orders placed on weekdays versus weekends.
* Determine the number of orders placed in each hour of the day.
* Count the orders for each toppings count.
* Identify the single location with the most orders.
* Split the orders between online and offline payment categories.

## Advanced

* Calculate the percentage share of total orders for each pizza type.
* Calculate the ratio of weekday orders to weekend orders.
* Determine how orders are distributed across the hours flagged as peak hours.
* Find how many orders were delayed, what percentage that is, and the average and maximum delays.
* Retrieve the 20 orders with the longest delays.
* Compare locations on order volume, average delivery time, average delay and delivery minutes per km.
* Analyse how average delay, delivery time and traffic impact differ by traffic level.
* Calculate the share of orders for each payment method.
* Determine which pizza sizes sell the most and the percentage share of each size.
* Measure delivery speed per km at each traffic level.
* Identify the 20 short-distance orders that took the longest per km.
* Summarise the headline numbers: total orders, average delivery duration, estimated duration, delay, distance, toppings and traffic impact.
* Compare the restaurants on orders, delivery time and delay.
